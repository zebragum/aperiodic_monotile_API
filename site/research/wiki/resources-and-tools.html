How to use this index

The numbered bibliography holds peer-reviewed and preprint research with stable citation anchors. This page catalogs everything else: official generators, open fabrication files, museum exhibits, workshop kits, games, OEIS sequences, journalism, and documented installations.

Tile(1,1) is not the same as Spectre. The straight-edged Tile(1,1) polygon admits a periodic tiling if reflected copies are mixed; Spectres are edge-modified, strictly chiral shapes. Many community repositories reuse the word “Spectre” for straight-edged outlines — verify the geometry before fabrication.[2]

An automated crawl maintains a machine-readable source registry for literature discovery. The lists below are human-curated and tiered: link freely, but check each site’s license before republishing assets.
